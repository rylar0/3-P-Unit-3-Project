Character Rarity

There are two rarities for a playable character in game: Five Star and Four Star, with a five str typically being stronger/more valuable than a four star. (Although that is not always the case).

To obtain a charcater you have to use the Gacha system where you pull using ingame currency called Primogems.

Five stars are typically obtained at soft or hard pity, which is 75 or 80+ pulls, respectively. Early five stars are anywhere under 70 pulls. Four stars, however, are never guaranteed no matter the pity.

  • Current amount of Five Stars: 70+
  • Current amount of Four Stars: 70+

Weapon Rarity

Weapons are a little different than characters because there are multiple ways to get them, either by pulling, exploring, or getting them as rewards.
Weapons can either be five stars, four stars, or three stars and their gacha functions work the same as characters.
Up until recently, weapons were really frustration and are still not worth pulling for people who like their money.
